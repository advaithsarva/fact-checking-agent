source: Historical reference summary on common Napoleon myths
---
Napoleon Bonaparte was not unusually short for his era. He was recorded at about 5 feet 7 inches in French units of the time, close to average height for a Frenchman of that period. The popular "Napoleon was short" belief is largely attributed to a mix-up between French and English measurement units and British political cartoons that exaggerated his height for satire.
